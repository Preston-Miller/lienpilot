Who should LienPilot loop in?

When an attorney needs something from the clinic, LienPilot emails the person who handles it and CCs the attorney. The same address can cover more than one role.

Staff who can ask about a patient

These people (and everyone above) can email LienPilot's address a question like "What's going on with Lucas Reed's lien?" and get an answer back. Anyone else gets no reply.

Upload your liens

A CSV with a header row. Required columns: patient_name, clinic_name, attorney_name, attorney_email. Optional: firm_name, date_of_service, lien_amount, status, last_contact_date, notes. This file replaces every lien currently in LienPilot, along with their history.
